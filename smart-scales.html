01 / The landscape

The landscape

A smart scale is four strain-gauge load cells under tempered glass, a bioelectrical impedance analysis circuit, and a Bluetooth radio. The load cells measure weight to within 50–100 grams. The BIA circuit sends a low, safe current through ITO-coated electrodes on the glass surface to estimate body-fat percentage, muscle mass, water percentage, bone mass, and a dozen other metrics that are, at best, directional rather than clinical. The Bluetooth module sends it all to a companion app on the user’s phone, which is where the brand actually lives.

Zhongshan is the old centre. Camry and its parent Senssun have been making scales there since 1975 — first mechanical, then digital, now connected — and the Zhongshan cluster’s tooling, load-cell, and glass-tempering supply base reflects five decades of specialisation. Shenzhen grafted the smart layer on top: BLE modules, app development, cloud backends, and the startup culture that spawned Yunmai (ex-Xiaomi founders) and Yolanda. The two cities sit 90 kilometres apart and function as one supply chain, with Zhongshan supplying the weighing heritage and Shenzhen supplying the connectivity and software.

The white-label app is the real product. Every ODM in this space offers SDK-level app reskinning so a brand can launch its own-branded iOS and Android app on top of the factory’s algorithm backend and cloud infrastructure. The brand’s customer sees a branded app with branded metrics; the ODM runs the servers, maintains the BIA algorithm, and pushes firmware updates. The hardware is nearly interchangeable across factories. The stickiness is in the app and the data — once a user has six months of body-composition history, switching to a competitor’s app means starting over.

The frontier is medical-grade body composition — segmental BIA with 8-electrode hand-and-foot arrays, FDA-listed devices, and integration with telehealth platforms. Transtek, publicly listed on the Shenzhen Stock Exchange as Lifesense Group (300562), is the ODM bridging consumer and medical, supplying Withings and Beurer alongside its own Lifesense brand while building ECG watches and remote-patient-monitoring systems.

01

Transtek 乐心医疗

Zhongshan

Transtek (Guangdong Transtek Medical Electronics, founded 2002, Torch High-Tech Industrial Development Zone, Zhongshan, SZSE: 300562 as Lifesense Group) is the largest and most vertically integrated smart-scale ODM in the PRD. A 25,000 m² facility with 2,000-plus employees and 80-plus R&D engineers, producing roughly 6 million units per year across smart body-fat scales, blood pressure monitors, blood glucose monitors, ECG watches, and wearables. Withings and Beurer are named as existing customers. ISO certified, FCC and CE compliant, with the Shenzhen Stock Exchange listing providing independent financial transparency.

Camry 凯米 / Senssun 三雄

Zhongshan

Camry (Zhongshan Camry Electronic, part of Guangdong Senssun Weighing Apparatus Group, SZSE: 002870, heritage dating to 1975, Baishawan Industrial Park, East District, Zhongshan) is the volume king — China’s household-scale market share and export leader for 16 consecutive years by the company’s own count. Six ISO 9001-certified factories, 107,000 m² combined floor space, 29 assembly lines, 3,000 workers, and 2.5 million pieces per month capacity. Over 450 models spanning bathroom scales, body-fat and hydration analysers, kitchen scales, and luggage scales, with a 50-person R&D team releasing 45 new designs per year. CE, GS, RoHS, LFGB, and REACH certified.

Canny 凯立电子

Zhongshan

Canny (Zhongshan Canny Electronics, founded 2010, Torch Development Zone, Zhongshan) bridges the old Zhongshan weighing heritage and the new Bluetooth-connected world. ISO 9001:2015, ISO 13485:2016, ISO 14001:2015, and BSCI certified, with product-level EMC, RoHS, PAH, LFGB, FCC, and UKCA compliance. Manufactures Renpho-branded body-fat scales per supplier listings — Renpho being a major US Amazon smart-scale brand — and also sells under its own SmartApple-CN and FitGo-CN lines. Bluetooth, kitchen, coffee, bathroom, body-fat, and luggage scales, with in-house module and app SDK design and custom weighing-sensor development. About 2 million pieces per year capacity.

Yolanda 优兰得科技

Shenzhen

Yolanda (Shenzhen Yolanda Technology, founded 2013) is the Shenzhen-native software-first approach to smart scales. ISO 9001, ISO 13485, BSCI, and CFDA certified, with product-level BLE, FCC, CE, KC, FDA, PSE, TELEC, RoHS, and REACH compliance. The differentiator is the “closed-loop” offering: hardware, companion app, and cloud data platform as one integrated package, with 13-metric body-composition analysis via BIA. Also produces kitchen scales, massage guns, and blood pressure monitors. Offers full OEM/ODM with software re-engineering — white-label app development — as a core service. Over 10 patents and 12 software copyrights.

Unique Scales 优尼科电子

Shenzhen · Longgang

Unique Scales (Shenzhen Unique Scales, founded 2002, Pingdi Street, Longgang District) is the high-volume Shenzhen factory with the broadest claimed client roster — Xiaomi, Anker, Lenovo, Etekcity, and Samsung, per the company’s own disclosure. A 35,000 m² factory with 21–28 production lines, 700–1,000-plus employees, and 12–25 million units per year capacity at 99.55% on-time delivery. ISO 9001, ISO 13485, ISO 14001, BSCI, and GRS certified, with TÜV Rheinland reports on file. WiFi (2.4G/5G) and Bluetooth (4.0–5.0) smart scales, BIA body-fat scales, solar-powered scales, kitchen scales, and baby scales, with Zigbee, Z-Wave, and Tuya smart-home integration. Over 40 proprietary body-fat algorithms with iOS and Android app development in-house.
