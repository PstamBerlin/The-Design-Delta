01 / The landscape

The landscape

A gimbal stabiliser is a three-axis motor platform that counteracts hand shake in real time. Three brushless DC motors (pitch, roll, yaw) driven by dedicated motor-driver ICs, typically Texas Instruments DRV8313-class triple half-H-bridge drivers, provide direct-drive torque without gearing backlash. A six-axis MEMS IMU (three-axis gyroscope plus three-axis accelerometer, commonly TDK InvenSense ICM-42686 or Bosch Sensortec alternatives) senses rotational disturbance, and a control loop running on an STM32-class ARM Cortex-M microcontroller fuses the sensor data through a Kalman or complementary filter and closes a PID loop to hold the camera steady. The entire stack, sensor, compute, motor, fits in a handle.

The form factors map to payloads. Phone gimbals are compact, foldable, with magnetic clamps and AI face-tracking, the volume segment. Camera gimbals (handheld, for DSLR and mirrorless) are larger, with follow-focus and zoom motor ports, the professional segment. Action-camera gimbals are ultra-compact and ruggedised. And integrated gimbal cameras combine the stabiliser and image sensor into a single pocketable unit. Every form factor uses the same core mechanism; the engineering differences are in payload capacity, motor torque curves, battery life and the sophistication of the control algorithm.

This industry clusters unusually. Zhiyun and Feiyu Tech are headquartered in Guilin, Guangxi, not the Pearl River Delta, but both maintain dedicated Shenzhen R&D and branch offices to tap the PRD's supply chain and talent. Hohem, Moza (Gudsen) and Funsnap are Shenzhen-native. The result is a Guilin–Shenzhen axis where algorithm and firmware IP develops in Guilin's lower-cost talent pool while hardware sourcing, prototyping and production happen through Shenzhen's component ecosystem. The open-source STorM32 gimbal controller (running on STM32) was widely cloned and adapted by Shenzhen ODMs, seeding the entire market.

The live frontier is AI-driven subject tracking and gesture control built into the gimbal firmware, plus integrated wireless microphones and LED fill lights turning the stabiliser into an all-in-one creator tool. Hohem and Funsnap explicitly accept OEM and ODM orders; for the others, direct enquiry to their business development contacts is required.

02 / Solution houses

The solution houses

01

Hohem 浩瀚卓越

Nanshan, Shenzhen

Hohem (Shenzhen Hohem Technology, founded 2014, Nanshan District, by founder Chen Min) is the ODM-ready house with scale. A national high-tech enterprise and a national-level "specialised and sophisticated small giant" (专精特新), it has its own factory with capacity for three to five million units a year and explicitly accepts OEM and ODM orders. In Best Buy's 800-plus US stores since 2016. AI face-tracking phone gimbals, action-camera stabilisers with roughly 70 per cent Amazon category share (company claim), and DSLR gimbals. The house that will build your gimbal under your name.

  • 2014founded
  • Nanshan, Shenzhenbase
  • 3–5M units/yearcapacity
  • Best Buy · 800+ storesretail
hohem.com
02

Moza Gudsen

Nanshan, Shenzhen

Moza (Shenzhen Gudsen Intelligent Technology, founded 2012, Nanshan District, Jiuxiang Ling Industrial Park) was started by automotive engineers and sim-racing enthusiasts, which explains the unusually deep mechatronic and motor-control R&D behind its camera stabilisers. The MOZA Air and AirCross lines feature in Sony Experience Centres, and the company partners with Atomos. It has also diversified into sim-racing hardware (MOZA Racing), demonstrating precision-motion engineering that transfers directly to gimbal control. The engineering-first house.

  • 2012founded
  • Nanshan, Shenzhenbase
  • Air · AirCross · Minilines
  • Sony · Atomospartners
gudsen.com
03

Zhiyun 智神信息

Guilin · Shenzhen

Zhiyun (Guilin Zhishen Information Technology, founded 2015, headquartered in Guilin's High-tech Zone with branch offices in Shenzhen, Hong Kong and the US) is the market-share leader, the brand that defined the category for many creators. The CRANE series (camera), WEEBILL (compact mirrorless), SMOOTH (phone) and more recently the FIVERAY and MOLUS on-camera LED lights. It led the drafting of China's first industry standard for camera stabilisers. Primarily a brand house rather than an open ODM, but its engineering depth and supply chain set the reference for the entire space.

  • 2015founded
  • Guilin · Shenzhenbase
  • CRANE · WEEBILL · SMOOTHlines
  • Industry standardauthor
zhiyun-tech.com
04

Feiyu Tech 飞宇

Guilin · Shenzhen

Feiyu Tech (Guilin Feiyu Technology, founded 2007, headquartered in Guilin's National High and New Tech Industry Park with a dedicated R&D and marketing centre in Shenzhen) is the pioneer, it started in drone flight controllers and pivoted into handheld stabilisers in 2013. Its G4 Pro smartphone gimbal sold through Apple retail (online and offline Apple Stores) starting 2015. The SCORP series for cameras, the Vimble and Pocket for phones and integrated gimbal cameras, CE/FCC/RoHS/UL certified. The house with the longest pedigree in stabilisation.

  • 2007founded
  • Guilin · Shenzhenbase
  • Apple retailsold
  • CE · FCC · ULcertified
feiyu-tech.com✉ service@feiyu-tech.com
05

Funsnap 逗映科技

Guangming, Shenzhen

Funsnap (Shenzhen Funsnap Technology, founded 2016, Guangming District, Hong'ao Industry Park, roughly 130 employees) is the compact, ODM-ready house. Its Alibaba listings explicitly advertise OEM/ODM with customised logo, confirming it takes private-label orders alongside its own Capture-series phone gimbals. It has expanded into wireless lavalier microphones (S6, M6, Mic Tok), positioning itself as a creator-accessories ODM. The house for a private-label phone gimbal or mic programme without a competing flagship brand in the way.

  • 2016founded
  • Guangming, Shenzhenbase
