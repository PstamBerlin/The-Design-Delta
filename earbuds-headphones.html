01 / The landscape

The landscape

In wireless audio the reference design is the chipset. An earbud house picks a system-on-chip, then builds the acoustics, the industrial design, the firmware and the sound tuning around it. The SoC decides almost everything downstream: codec support (SBC, AAC, aptX, LDAC), Bluetooth version, power budget, and how good the noise cancelling can get. The names to know are Bestechnic (BES), Airoha, Jieli (JL) and Actions for the volume tiers, and Qualcomm QCC at the premium end.

The current battleground is noise. ANC (active noise cancelling) quiets the world around you; ENC (environmental noise cancelling) cleans up your voice on calls. Both lean heavily on the SoC and the microphone array, and both are where a good house earns its margin over a generic assembler. Ask any supplier which silicon they run and why, an answer separates a design house from a box-shifter.

Geography is a corridor, not a point. The audio cluster runs across Shenzhen (Bao'an), Dongguan and Huizhou, an hour's drive end to end, sharing acoustic engineers, mould shops and component supply. Houses offer OEM, ODM and white label from catalogues of twenty-plus ready models, carrying FCC, CE and RoHS plus Bluetooth SIG qualification for export.

The apex of this same supply chain is AirPods, assembled by Luxshare in Dongguan and acoustically driven by GoerTek in Weifang, Shandong, both EMS giants, not catalogue houses you can hire. The brands you actually compete with (Anker / Soundcore, Edifier, QCY, 1MORE) sit one layer above the hireable ODMs below.

02 / Solution houses

The solution houses

01

Enle BJBJ

Shenzhen · Bao'an (+ Dongguan)

Shenzhen Enle Industry, trading as BJBJ (“Be Joyful”), is a design-led TWS and wireless-speaker ODM founded around 2015. It runs a 10,000 m²-plus, ISO 9001 / BSCI plant with its own anechoic chamber and testing lab, 100-plus design patents and roughly 600,000 pieces a month. Mid-to-high-end ANC and ENC earbuds across BES, Airoha and Jieli platforms, with a deep ready catalogue for private label. HQ in Bao'an, a second factory in Dongguan.

Roman Technology

Shenzhen · Longgang

Shenzhen Roman Technology (founded 2008) is a national high-tech enterprise and one of the larger Bluetooth-audio houses, with a smart factory rated above one million units a month, an independent acoustic laboratory and 240-plus patents. Beyond standard TWS it is strong in open-ear and bone-conduction sport audio, on OEM/ODM terms with a broad low-MOQ model range. It runs as both an own-brand and a contract house.

Wellyp

Huizhou

Wellyp Technology (founded 2004 in Huizhou, the eastern point of the Shenzhen–Huizhou–Dongguan triangle) is a turnkey audio ODM spanning TWS earbuds, ANC headphones and wired gaming headsets. BSCI/Sedex-audited, with its own moulding and assembly, an audiology engineering team, 3–7 day sampling and small-MOQ private label. The headset-and-gaming-leaning house of the set.

Gorsun

Dongguan · Chashan

Dongguan Gorsun Electronics (since 2008, Chashan Town) is a headphones-first ODM: wired and Bluetooth over-ear and on-ear headphones, volume-limited kids headphones, open-ear sport headsets and TWS, with its own tooling and patents and a Shenzhen-based R&D team. ISO 9001, 200-plus staff, three to four new models a quarter.
