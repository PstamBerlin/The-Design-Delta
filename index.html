Who designs
what.谁来设计

China doesn't design everywhere. It designs somewhere specific.

Most hardware isn't drawn from scratch. You go to a house that already owns the reference platform and the factory relationships, and you bring the brand. This is a practical field guide to those houses across the Pearl River Delta, who designs what, product by product, where they cluster, and how to reach them.
