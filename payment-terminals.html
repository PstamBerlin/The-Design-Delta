01 / The landscape

The landscape

China is the workshop of the world's checkout counter. Two Shenzhen firms, PAX and NEXGO, rank among the largest payment-terminal makers on earth by units shipped, between them tens of millions of devices across more than a hundred countries.

The product has moved up the stack. It began as a countertop EFT-POS box with a PIN pad; since PAX shipped its first Android smart terminal in 2016, the category has become Android SmartPOS, a touchscreen computer with camera, scanner and an app store, spanning mPOS dongles, handhelds, desktop ECRs and facial-recognition units.

The moat is certification, not plastic. Anyone can mould an Android tablet. What these houses own is the secure platform: EMV scheme approval, PCI PTS tamper-resistant hardware, the newer PCI MPoC / SoftPOS standards, secure key injection, and the patience to carry a design through every regional acquirer's lab. That certified core, plus global certification support, is what you are really paying for.

Geography: Shenzhen holds the dense core (PAX, NEXGO); Foshan is the Pearl-River sibling, strong in biometric and face-recognition POS and self-service kiosks. The other Chinese majors sit outside the Delta, Newland and Landi in Fuzhou, Sunmi in Shanghai, and are out of scope here.

02 / Solution houses

The solution houses

01

PAX Technology

Shenzhen

Founded in Shenzhen in 2000 and listed in Hong Kong (00327), PAX is, by Nilson's count, the world's second-largest payment-terminal provider. The range covers PIN pads, countertop and portable terminals, the A-series Android SmartPOS line, and self-service kiosks, all on the open PAXSTORE app platform. An early UnionPay and Bank of China supplier, with multiple iF and Red Dot design awards.

NEXGO XGD

Shenzhen

Shenzhen Xinguodu (XGD), founded 2001 and listed on Shenzhen's GEM board (300130), built China's first wireless POS and first colour-screen POS, and an early PCI-passing wireless terminal. It cites 25 to 38 million units deployed across 80-plus countries: smart, mobile, desktop and mini POS plus PIN pads, with in-house firmware and EMC/PCI certification support, and an early PCI MPoC SoftPOS approval. NEXGO is the group's global brand.

Telpo Telepower

Foshan · Nanhai

Telepower Communication, trading as Telpo, was founded in 1999 in Nanhai district, Foshan, where it opened a new 17-storey headquarters in 2025. Across 300-plus ODM projects in 100-plus countries it builds smart and EFT-POS terminals, cash registers, biometric and face-recognition terminals, bus-ticketing machines and kiosks, with in-house EMC, drop-test and optical labs and EMV/NFC/QR across global schemes.
