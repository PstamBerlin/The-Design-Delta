01 / The landscape

The landscape

A smart lock welds three disciplines to one door. There is the mechanism (a mortise lock body and a motor-and-clutch that throws the bolt), the credential stack (how you prove who you are), and the cloud (how it talks to a phone). The reference design you buy is really a choice of which credential modules and which app platform a house has already integrated, the casing is the easy part.

The mechanism is the old trade. Mortise sizes (5050, 6085 and the European DIN profile), anti-drill panels, C-grade cylinders and salt-spray-rated housings come from a lineage that predates electronics by decades. Zhongshan, branded the country's Lock Capital, has stamped and machined this hardware for a generation; Shenzhen then bolts the electronics on. Most smart locks are that marriage.

Credentials are where the catalogue competes: fingerprint (semiconductor FPC or optical), a PIN keypad, an RFID/NFC card (13.56MHz Mifare), and the premium tiers now 3D face and palm vein, with a mechanical keyway and a USB-C jump-start as the fallback. The connectivity, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, Zigbee and the emerging Matter-over-Thread, almost always rides a platform: Tuya (Smart Life) or TTLock / TTHotel. Few houses write their own cloud; they license one of those two.

Security is a standards game: EU EN 14846 plus RED for the radio, US ANSI/BHMA grading and UL, and China's Public Security Bureau (GA) mark. The houses below are the hireable layer beneath the brands (Kaadas, Dessmann, Aqara, Lockin, Philips, Yale and August under Assa Abloy). The tell of the rebadge: one Tuya-platform fingerprint lock sells under dozens of names.

Mendock Olang

Zhongshan · Xiaolan

MENDOCK (Guangdong Olang Security Technology), in Xiaolan Town, Zhongshan, the country's lock capital, brings the mechanical half. Since 2007 it has made precision lock cases, cylinders, hinges and the smart locks built around them, across four factories and 45,000 m², 300-plus staff and 30-plus patents on lock mechanics and anti-theft. Turnkey from mechanical design, injection and metal stamping through PCB, firmware and app, with IP65 and UL testing in house.

Sinovo Technologies

Shenzhen

Shenzhen Sinovo Technologies (founded 2013, a national high-tech enterprise, TÜV-verified) is the electronics-and-biometrics end. Built on wireless and MCU/ARM expertise, it spans fingerprint, 3D face, camera/peephole, push-pull, deadbolt, cylinder and glass-door locks, on Tuya, TTLock and Ekey. The volume tells the story: in 2024 it shipped roughly 3.7 million PCB assemblies and 8.56 million fingerprint modules. Broad OEM/ODM with TÜV-grade QC.

Be-Tech Security

Foshan · Shunde

Guangdong Be-Tech Security Systems (Shunde, Foshan, with a Hong Kong office) is the hospitality and commercial heavyweight: a 56,000 m²-plus maker of hotel, office and residential locks. It impact- and salt-spray-tests lock cases in its own lab and certifies to CE, RoHS, UL and the US ANSI/BHMA grade, citing a sub-0.1% failure rate. The house to talk to when a project needs graded, audited hardware at scale.
