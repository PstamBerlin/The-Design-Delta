01 / The landscape

The landscape

Smart pet technology is four product pillars built on the same Shenzhen electronics supply chain. A smart feeder portions kibble on a WiFi-controlled schedule via auger or turntable mechanism, increasingly bundled with a 1080p CMOS camera and two-way audio so the owner can watch and talk. A GPS tracker combines GPS, WiFi positioning and LBS cell-tower triangulation on a 4G LTE or NB-IoT radio, strapped to a collar, reporting location to a companion app via monthly SIM subscription. A self-cleaning litter box uses motorised raking or rotating-globe sifting with weight sensors for multi-cat identification and waste-level alerts. A pet camera sits on a shelf or rolls autonomously through the home, dispensing treats and tracking the animal with AI behaviour recognition.

The enabling layer is Shenzhen’s IoT platform ecosystem, especially Tuya Smart (NYSE: TUYA). A factory can ship a WiFi-connected feeder with a branded companion app without building cloud infrastructure from scratch, because Tuya supplies the pre-certified wireless module, the cloud backend, and the white-label app shell. This is why dozens of PRD factories offer “smart” feeders that look different on the shelf but share the same connectivity stack underneath. The barrier to entry is the hardware and the mould, not the software.

The brand-factory dynamic mirrors consumer electronics at large. DTC brands like Petlibro, dozens of Amazon private-label sellers, and retail chains commission hardware from PRD ODMs that handle industrial design, mechanical and electrical engineering, firmware, tooling, and assembly under one roof. The brand owns the listing, the app skin, and the customer. Some factories — Dogness, Enabot — also run their own consumer brands alongside OEM production, competing with their own clients in a dynamic the industry treats as normal.

The frontier is AI-powered pet monitoring: cameras with behaviour recognition that distinguish play from distress, health tracking from litter-box weight sensors that flag irregular patterns, and companion robots that move autonomously through the home. Enabot’s EBO line, backed by Sequoia Capital China, points toward a convergence of pet tech and home robotics, using the pet as the beachhead into a larger family-companion-robot market.

02 / Solution houses

The solution houses

01

Dogness 铠威电子

Dongguan

Dogness (Dongguan Kaiwei Electronics, NASDAQ: DOGZ, founded 2003) is the only publicly traded pure-play pet-tech manufacturer in this space, with SEC filings that make its operations independently verifiable. The company designs and manufactures smart feeders with integrated cameras, pet health and hygiene devices, GPS-enabled collars, and leash systems across its own Dogness brand and private-label OEM programmes. A Dongguan headquarters with a materials sub-factory in Zhangzhou, Fujian, and a subsidiary — Dogness Intelligent Technology (DITC, established 2018) — dedicated to IoT and app development for its connected product lines.

Petwant 前海元味科技

Shenzhen · Bao'an

Petwant (Shenzhen Qianhai Yuanwei Technology, founded 2003, Bao’an District with a factory in Dongguan) claims to have built the first-ever three-meal automatic pet feeder. Now spanning smart feeders, water fountains, cat litter boxes, grooming tools, and pet toys, with 41 registered IP rights and over 100 completed OEM/ODM projects across 80-plus countries. ISO 9001, BSCI, SEDEX, and SMETA certified, with CE, FCC, RoHS and REACH compliance built in. States it has worked with Royal Canin, Bissell, Anker, Chewy, and Trixie.

TIZE 泰泽科技

TIZE (Shenzhen TIZE Technology, founded 2011, Bao’an District) is a wearable-pet-tech specialist — GPS pet trackers, bark-control and training collars, ultrasonic repellers, LED collars and harnesses, and pet water fountains. A B2B-focused operation that exports to global distributors and private-label brands under white-label terms, with CE, FCC, and RoHS compliance across its product lines. Petoneer 宠尔科技

Shenzhen · Longhua

Petoneer (founded 2017, Longhua District, Guanlan) is a Tuya Smart technology partner that builds its entire WiFi and BLE connectivity stack on Tuya’s IoT platform — certified modules, cloud infrastructure, and app framework included. Known for its Fresco smart water fountain line with multi-stage filtration, plus smart feeders, interactive toys, UV sanitising pens, and odour eliminators. Retail presence via Central (Thailand), Pet Tec (Australia), and growing international distribution. A good example of how Tuya lowers the barrier to “smart” for smaller PRD factories.

Enabot 赋之科技

Shenzhen

Enabot (Fuzhi Technology (Shenzhen), founded 2018) is the AI companion-robot play in this space, backed by Sequoia Capital China (HSG), Longfor Capital, and Jihe Ventures. Its EBO line — EBO S, EBO Air, EBO X — are mobile home-monitoring robots with SLAM navigation, and its dedicated ROLA pet sub-brand includes the PetPal companion robot with treat dispensing, a GPS pet-tracking camera, and a smart fountain. Over one million users across 160-plus countries, with retail partnerships including Sam’s Club and B&H Photo. FCC Class B certified, the strongest pure-play in AI pet cameras and companion robots among PRD manufacturers.
