01 / The landscape

The landscape

Guangdong is the world's largest home appliance manufacturing base, and the concentration inside it is tighter than most people expect. Two places carry most of it: Shunde district in Foshan, where Midea has its headquarters in Beijiao town, and Zhuhai, where Gree does. The scale is hard to hold in the head. Midea turned over 456.5 billion yuan in the 2025 financial year, roughly 66 billion dollars, on net profit of 43.9 billion, and it is the largest home appliance company on earth as well as the largest maker of microwave ovens and a substantial OEM for brands that would rather you did not know. Gree, on its own, has capacity for 27 million residential air conditioners a year.

In an air conditioner, a refrigerator or a heat pump, the part that decides the product is the compressor. It sets the efficiency, and therefore the energy label; it sets the noise floor; and it is the largest single cost in the bill of materials. Nobody outside a handful of firms designs one. You select one, and that selection places your product in a tier. This is the closest thing this category has to a reference design, and the delta owns it outright: GMCC in Shunde builds more than a hundred million rotary compressors a year and has ranked first in the world in air conditioner rotary compressor output and sales since 2006. When people say Guangdong makes the world's air conditioning, this is the layer they are actually describing.

The gate is chemistry, and it has a date. Regulation (EU) 2024/573, the recast F-gas rules, was published on 20 February 2024 and entered into force on 11 March 2024. From 1 January 2027, plug-in room air conditioners, monoblock and other self-contained air conditioning equipment and self-contained heat pumps rated up to and including 12kW may not be placed on the EU market if they contain fluorinated greenhouse gases with a global warming potential of 150 or more. From 1 January 2033 the same threshold closes on split systems above 12kW. The practical effect is the retirement of the refrigerants this industry was built around, in favour of A2L blends such as R-454B and R-32 or, increasingly, hydrocarbons.

The winner among those is R-290, which is propane, with a global warming potential of 3, and it became viable partly through a standards change rather than a chemical one: EN IEC 60335-2-89:2022 raised the permitted charge from 150 grams to 500 grams, which is the difference between a novelty and a product. But propane burns. Designing around it means leak detection, control of ignition sources, sealed electrical compartments, revised service procedures and different transport rules, which is a redesign of the machine rather than a change of fill. Treat the refrigerant as an architectural decision made at concept stage. Ask a supplier which refrigerant a platform was designed for rather than which one it can be charged with, and ask whether the compressor itself is rated for hydrocarbons. And note the delta's structural advantage here: because Shunde and Zhuhai own the compressor as well as the appliance, they can move the entire stack at once in a way an assembler buying compressors on the open market cannot.

For export, the appliance gates are heavier than the electronics ones elsewhere in this guide. Beyond CE and UKCA there is ecodesign and energy labelling, where the class you achieve is a commercial fact rather than a compliance detail, the EN IEC 60335 safety series with its specific parts for refrigerating appliances and heat pumps, and the F-gas timetable above, which also carries quota, servicing, leak-checking and certification obligations on the operator side. EU ecodesign rules already oblige spare part availability and repair information for several appliance classes, so parts strategy and documentation belong in the programme from the start. For the United States, note separately that refrigerant transitions there run on their own schedule and do not map cleanly onto the European dates.

02 / Solution houses

The solution houses

01

Midea 美的集团

Foshan · Shunde

Headquartered in Beijiao town in Shunde and listed in Shenzhen as 000333 since 2013, Midea is the largest home appliance company in the world: 456.5 billion yuan of revenue in the 2025 financial year, about 66 billion dollars, with 43.9 billion yuan of net profit and roughly 40 percent of sales outside China. The range covers refrigeration, laundry, large cooking, small kitchen, water and floor care, and it is the world's largest microwave oven manufacturer as well as a significant OEM for other brands. It is also no longer only an appliance company: the 2017 acquisition of KUKA, the German industrial robot maker, put it in automation, which is worth remembering when you meet it as a supplier rather than a competitor.

02

Gree 格力电器

Zhuhai

Gree Electric Appliances of Zhuhai, established 1991 and listed as 000651, is the world's largest air conditioner manufacturer and the most specialised house on this page. Around 90,000 employees and fifteen production bases, in China and also Vietnam, Pakistan and Brazil, support annual capacity of some 27 million residential and 2 million commercial air conditioners. Where Midea spread across every appliance category and then into robotics, Gree went deep into one product and built its own compressor capability to support it, which is the reason it can argue about efficiency at the component level rather than the assembly level. If the requirement is air conditioning specifically, this is the specialist.

03

Galanz 格兰仕

Foshan · Zhongshan

Founded 1978, which makes it older than the industry it now leads, Galanz runs its production and research across Foshan and Zhongshan and holds the world's largest microwave oven research and manufacturing base. More than 50,000 employees, some 8 million air conditioner units and 2 million compressors a year, exporting to over 100 countries. Its historical role is worth knowing because it explains the pricing of a whole product class: Galanz drove the microwave from a middle-class luxury to a commodity through sheer scale and vertical integration, and it did the same trick again in other categories afterwards. The house to study when the question is how a category's floor price got set.

04

GMCC 美芝

Foshan · Shunde

The component layer, and the one that actually decides the product. Guangdong Meizhi Compressor, trading as GMCC, was founded in 1995, sits within the Midea group and operates from Shunde with more than 10,000 employees across two plant areas totalling 340,000 square metres. It builds over a hundred million rotary compressors a year for air conditioners, freezers, heat pump water heaters, dehumidifiers, dryers and refrigerated transport, and has been first in the world for air conditioner rotary compressor output and sales since 2006. This is the house to ask about hydrocarbon compatibility before anyone shows you a finished unit, because a compressor not rated for propane makes the rest of the 2027 conversation academic.
