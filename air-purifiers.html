01 / The landscape

The landscape

An air purifier is a fan, a filter stack, a sensor array, and increasingly a WiFi module in a housing. The filter stack does the work: a pre-filter catches hair and coarse dust, a true HEPA layer (H13 or H14 grade, 99.97% capture at 0.3 microns) traps fine particulate matter, and an activated-carbon bed adsorbs volatile organic compounds and odours. Some designs add UV-C germicidal lamps or negative-ion generators, though the better engineers debate whether the ozone by-products of ionisation outweigh the benefit — at least one major PRD manufacturer publicly avoids UV-C on those grounds.

Foshan’s Shunde District has been called China’s Home Appliance City for decades. Over 3,000 appliance enterprises clustered around Midea and Galanz built the injection-moulding, motor-winding, and mould-tooling supply base that air-purifier ODMs now draw on directly. Shenzhen adds the electronics layer: PM2.5 and VOC sensor modules, Bluetooth and WiFi connectivity, Tuya IoT integration, and the rapid-prototyping infrastructure of Huaqiangbei. Guangzhou’s Nansha District, though technically a separate municipality, functions as part of the same integrated logistics and port network, and hosts large full-stack ODMs like Olansi. The region offers vertical integration — filter media, injection-moulded housings, BLDC and AC motors, and final assembly can all be sourced within a 100-kilometre radius.

The business model has two revenue streams: the hardware sale and the recurring filter replacement. Nearly every ODM designs proprietary filter-cartridge geometries for each OEM client, because the downstream brand makes its margin on subscription filter sales, not the purifier itself. This is why a brand rarely shares a filter format with a competitor — the lock-in is by design, and the ODM tools a new filter mould for each programme. The factory sells the razor; the brand sells the blades.

CADR — Clean Air Delivery Rate — is the number Western buyers scrutinise most, and the better PRD factories now run in-house CADR testing labs rather than outsourcing to third parties. Post-COVID demand cooled from 2021–2022 peaks but stabilised around wildfire-smoke awareness, allergen management, and the built-in filter-replacement annuity that makes the category sticky once the hardware is placed.

01

Olansi 欧兰斯

Guangzhou · Nansha

Olansi (Olansi Healthcare, Guangzhou, legally registered as Guangzhou Olansi Water Treatment Equipment Co., founded 2009, Nansha District) is the full-stack giant in this space. A 60,000 m² campus with its own injection-mould, filter-media, and assembly factories, producing 3,000 units per day. An in-house Evaluation Centre spans 450 m² with 11 labs and over 100 instruments for CADR and performance testing. ISO 9001:2015, CE, CB, RoHS, SASO, CQC, and CCC certified. Claims to supply product lines for Philips, Honeywell, and Haier private-label programmes, plus 10 of the top 20 global MLM health brands. Over 100 patents across air purifiers, water purifiers, and hydrogen-water generators.

Airmate 艾美特

Shenzhen · Bao'an

Airmate (Airmate Electrical (Shenzhen), originally Tung Fu Electric, founded in Taiwan in 1973, Shenzhen production plant established 1991 in Shiyan, Bao’an District) carries a legacy few PRD factories can match. Decades as an OEM manufacturer for Sanyo, Toshiba, Samsung, LG, and SEB (France) before building its own AIRMATE consumer brand. True HEPA (99.9% at 0.3 μm) plus activated carbon, with a deliberate decision to avoid UV-C — the company publicly cites ozone and secondary-pollution concerns. Also makes fans, heaters, and humidifiers. Now sells under its own brand via Amazon US alongside ongoing OEM programmes.

WSTA 万速科技 / Vansu

Shenzhen · Longhua

WSTA (Shenzhen Vansu Technology, founded 2013, Longhua District, part of the Wangcheng Group founded 1997) is a mid-scale specialist with in-house CADR, noise, and safety testing labs that let it self-certify performance before third-party submission. ISO 9001, with products carrying CE (LVD/EMC/RoHS), FCC, ETL/cETL, and PSE certifications. Produces HEPA air purifiers, ozone generators, UV sterilisers, and humidifiers across floor, desktop, car, and wearable formats. Capacity of 60,000 to 150,000 units per month with 14 R&D engineers and an annual output around US$5–10 million.

Wintek 万事泰科技

Foshan · Shunde

Wintek (Guangdong Wintek Science and Technology, Xingtan Town, Shunde District, Foshan, current entity established 2019 from the earlier WEIHE ventilation business with roots to the mid-1990s) bridges ventilation and purification. Its core is exhaust fans, inline fans, and fresh-air ventilators, but it has expanded into air purifiers with integrated ventilation — including a smart exhaust fan with built-in Amazon Alexa launched in 2021. ISO 9001:2015, with products passing IEC CB, CE, GS, ETL, HVI, RoHS, GCC, and SAA. 2023 revenue exceeded RMB 300 million with 20% year-on-year growth, over 300 manufacturing staff, and roughly 10% of revenue reinvested in R&D.

Purecare 普凯电器

Purecare (Foshan Purecare Electric Technology, founded 2016, Ronggui, Shunde District) is a focused air-purifier ODM with in-house CADR testing and new-product ageing rooms. CE, ISO, RoHS, ETL, CARB, and FCC certified across its product lines. Builds desk, wall-mount, and floor air purifiers in HEPA and UV-C lamp variants, plus combination humidifier-purifiers and dehumidifiers, with a dedicated BLDC-motor production line. Reports 100-plus OEM/ODM customers across the USA, Germany, Spain, Malaysia, Thailand, Korea, and India, and is a regular Canton Fair exhibitor.
